## 2. Key product context
 
### Back in Stock (BIS)
 
Core value prop: Capture lost demand when products sell out — notify customers when restocked.
 
Pricing tiers (current):
- Free plan (10 notifications/month, with limits)
- Lite: $19/mo (50 notifications)
- Medium: $39/mo (500 notifications)
- Large: $99/mo (50,000 notifications) — cheapest per-notification at scale in category
- Enterprise: $199–$999/mo
Differentiators vs. competitors:
- Predictable flat-fee pricing — no per-SMS surprises
- Free plan with NO Amp branding (most competitors brand their free plans)
- Best $/notification at scale ($99 = 50K notifications = $0.002 each)
Current performance (as of May 2026):
- Monthly installs: 345-578 (down 56% YoY from 797)
- Monthly premium trials: 97-266 (down 34% from 146)
- Trial-to-paid conversion: ~30% (down from peak 88%)
- Monthly churn: ~190 customers / 7-11% range
- ARR: ~$1.13M (down from $1.46M peak)
- 18.1% of new installs are 50+ order merchants
- 63.7% of existing paying customers are 50+ order merchants (the real quality)
Top features:
- Email, SMS, and push notifications
- Multi-language support
- Pre-order capability
- Klaviyo / Mailchimp / Postscript integrations
- Multi-location warehouse support (Markets in development)
- Smart delivery controls (delay after restock, minimum inventory limit, notification policy)
Currently building:
- Shopify Markets support (PRD complete — multi-warehouse/region-aware notifications)
- Inline button customization with live preview (recently launched)
### Slide Cart
 
Core value prop: Increase AOV at the moment customers decide to buy — cart-level upsells, rewards, free gifts.
 
Pricing tiers (current):
- Free plan
- $19/mo entry (~50 notifications-equivalent)
- $69/mo (5,000 notifications)
- $99/mo (50,000 notifications)
- New ARPA in May 2026: $44 (highest ever — signals underpricing)
- Average existing ARPA: $35-36
Current performance (May 2026):
- ~2,660 paying customers (down from 3,704 peak)
- ARR: ~$1.13M (May projection: -0.6% MoM, flat trajectory)
- Churn collapsed to -3.26% in May 2026 (best month in 54 months)
- 17.6% of new installs are 50+ order merchants
- 35.9% of existing paying customers are 50+ order merchants
- 60% of high-volume paying customers haven't opened the app in 30 days (massive dormant pool)
- Rating: ★5.0
Top features:
- Cart upsell & cross-sell (frequently bought together)
- BOGO (buy X get Y)
- Free gifts (MOQ or cart value triggered)
- Tiered rewards / progress bar
- Sticky cart drawer
- Discount box (in-cart coupon redemption)
- Shipping protection (single-click add-on, recent launch)
- Full visual customization, custom CSS, multi-language

## 4. Strategic context
 
### Where Amp wins
- Flat-fee predictability at scale (vs per-SMS metered competitors)
- Suite economics — only player with 4 connected apps (BIS, Slide Cart, Bundles, Lifetimely)
- No branding on free plans (industry-rare)
- High existing-customer quality (63.7% / 35.9% are 50+ order merchants)
- 5.0 ratings on Slide Cart & Bundles, ★4.9 on BIS
### Where Amp is losing
- Install funnel quality: 55% of BIS installs are zero-order merchants
- App Store ranking: #4-5 in both categories — top 3 compound advantages
- Feature parity: Shopify Markets support gap drove 20+ recent merchant complaints
- Listing copy: feature-name framing vs competitors' outcome framing
- Trial length: 7-day BIS trial vs 14-day category norm
### Core strategic thesis (Anoop's working hypothesis)
 
Stop competing on install volume. Compete on install quality. Be the app that high-volume DTC and Plus merchants choose specifically because Amp serves them better. Slower top-line growth but:
- Higher ARPA
- Lower churn
- Better unit economics for paid acquisition
- Defensible position as competitors race for breadth

### Dormant high-value segment (current focus)
 
3,237 Slide Cart paying customers with 50+ orders/month who haven't opened the app in 30 days. These are:
- Churn risk (passive customers churn fast)
- Cross-sell opportunity (high-quality, already trust Amp)
- Re-engagement opportunity (recover lost product engagement)

## 10. Glossary
 
- BIS: Back in Stock (Amp's restock alert app)
- BFS: Built for Shopify (Shopify's app quality badge)
- AOV: Average Order Value
- OOS: Out of stock
- DTC: Direct-to-consumer
- Plus: Shopify Plus (enterprise tier)
- 3PL: Third-party logistics (used for multi-warehouse setups)
- ARPA: Average revenue per account
- ARR / MRR: Annual / Monthly recurring revenue
- Lifetimely: Amp's profit & LTV analytics app
- Markets: Shopify's multi-region/multi-warehouse framework
- Klaviyo / Postscript: Email and SMS marketing platforms commonly integrated by BIS users
- Helpscout: Amp's customer support ticketing system
- Slite: Amp's internal documentation tool

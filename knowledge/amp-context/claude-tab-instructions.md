# Using Claude Tab for Extended Knowledge

Frosty has access to a Claude tab in the browser that contains detailed AMP documentation and context files that are too large to pre-load.

## When to Use the Claude Tab

- When you need detailed information about AMP apps (BIS, Preorder, Bundles, Slide Cart, etc.) that isn't in your pre-loaded knowledge files
- When you encounter an unfamiliar issue and need deeper context
- When you need to understand AMP's internal APIs, webhooks, or integration details
- When the pre-loaded knowledge isn't enough to solve the problem

## How to Use It

1. Use `list_pages` to find the Claude tab (look for "claude.ai" or "Claude" in the tab titles)
2. `select_page` to switch to the Claude tab
3. Type your question in Claude's input field and submit it
4. Wait for Claude to respond
5. Read Claude's response to get the context you need
6. `select_page` back to your working tab and continue

## Tips

- Be specific with your questions to Claude — "What is the DOM structure of the BIS modal?" is better than "tell me about BIS"
- **Read Claude's ENTIRE response** — do not skim or cherry-pick. Scroll through the full answer and read everything before switching back. Claude's responses often contain critical details at the end.
- You can ask Claude multiple questions in one visit
- Always switch back to your working tab after getting the answer
- Use Claude for knowledge lookups, not for fixing — you do the fixing
